1. ONE TRAINING SEED  (reviewer attack #1)
   Analysis done (analysis_results.txt, part B; figure D grey band):
   - KID of the same run at epochs 40/41/42/44 varies by 11.6 % (relative SD), 3.2x the evaluation (bootstrap) SD.
     This is used as a proxy for training variability: an effect must exceed ~23 % (2 checkpoint SDs) to be robust.
   - Wording (per ChatGPT review): this is variation WITHIN ONE training trajectory, not seed-to-seed variance.
     Say "large relative to observed checkpoint variation", never "robust across seeds" or "training variance is 11.6 %".
   - Large relative to checkpoint variation: every FP arm (+54 % to +158 %), 4 of 5 FP-derived regularisers (+31 % to +36 %),
     dynamic thresholding (+35 % to +50 %), the acquisition noise layer (+42 %). All of these are HARMFUL effects,
     so the negative conclusions do not depend on the seed.
   - Within observed checkpoint variation: weight averaging (-8.6 %), best-of-2 (-8.9 %), spectral norm r2b (+4 %). These are within training noise;
     report them as "consistent but small, not distinguishable from checkpoint variability".
   Writing: call all CIs "evaluation-level uncertainty (over generated and reference images)", never "model uncertainty".

2. "PHYSICS" AT 256 px / AFTER PROCESSING  (attack #2)
   Analysis done (figure H): CBIS noise-level function is U-shaped and highest in dark regions (Poisson-Gaussian fit
   R2 0.50) - not photon-noise-like. CMMD rises with brightness up to mid intensity, then flattens/falls (linear R2 0.76,
   quadratic 0.98). Wording to use: "a simple detector-level Poisson-Gaussian model is incompatible with the processed
   256 px CBIS-DDSM representation and approximately compatible with CMMD" - NOT "film has no acquisition physics".
   Still needed (S9, below): measured points for CMMD, and a downsampling sweep on CMMD originals.

3. METRIC VALIDITY (KID-mammo uses a classifier trained on the same data)  (attack #3)
   Already in hand: two independent feature spaces (DINOv2, and Inception added in S8); validation MSE predicts KID
   across 10 models (Spearman 0.89, p 0.0005) while the FP residual does not (0.18, p 0.63) - figure C.
   Context number to report: real-vs-real KID floor = 0.0055; the baseline's generated KID ~0.49 is ~90x the floor.
   Every intervention moves KID by 0.04-0.8 while the gap to real data stays ~0.49 - say this plainly (weak base model).

4. TWO DATASETS ARE NOT TWO INDEPENDENT VALIDATIONS  (attack #4)
   Writing: frame CBIS (digitised film) vs CMMD (digital FFDM) as a deliberate contrast of acquisition representations,
   not as replication of the same process. Figure H is the evidence for why they differ.

5. FP UNDER-POWERED / MIS-MATCHED  (attack #5)
   Analysis done (figures E, F): |lambda g_FP| / |g_base| = 0.41 / 1.38 / 2.74; mean cos(base, FP) 0.001-0.005;
   clipped steps 89-97 % vs 77 % in the lambda-0 control; FP gradient SNR 0.04-0.12 per sample vs 0.17 for the
   denoising loss per micro-batch; SNR 1 needs 69-620 samples per step (>= 35 at the upper CI).
   Wording: "the FP constraint was poorly conditioned relative to the denoising objective at the available batch size".

6. MEMORISATION / DIVERSITY  (ChatGPT point 12)
   Analysis done (part I): no near-copies - 0.0 % of generated images are closer to a training image than the 5th
   percentile of real-to-real nearest-neighbour distances, in both feature spaces; generated->real NN distances are
   1.22x (mammo) and 1.75x (DINOv2) the real->real ones. S4a pixel-level memorisation was 0.000 for every arm.
   Interpretation: the generator is not copying; it is far from the real manifold (consistent with recall ~0).

7b. HIGH-FREQUENCY ENERGY (exploratory, figure G2): over 12 sets, MORE fine-detail energy goes with WORSE KID
   (Spearman +0.49, p 0.11). So neither saturation nor lost fine detail explains the KID gap on its own.

7. SATURATION  (ChatGPT point 13)
   Analysis done (figure G): over 10 image sets, saturation and KID are not positively related (Spearman -0.36, p 0.31);
   the three sets with saturation at or below the real value (1.3 %) all have WORSE KID. Fixing a visible artefact moved
   the distribution away from the real data.
